## Four-way classification

The authoritative rows are `blueprint.json.feature_classification`; regenerate this view after edits.

### Required now

- one named Mineflayer NPC and one authorized principal;
- addressed text chat and bounded typed perception;
- one structured model decision;
- one runtime-owned utterance validation/submission path;
- asynchronous `follow_player`, `stop_behavior`, and emergency stop;
- capability-appropriate movement evidence and honest uncertainty;
- connected suspension and zero model activity during absence;
- stable Agent/Principal/World/Epoch and causal IDs;
- complete durable events and one deterministic encounter;
- minimal SQLite, integrity, backup/restore/reset, shutdown/restart/reconciliation/retrieval;
- causal logs/doctor/evidence export;
- fixed experience/retrieval tests and three repeated owner-reviewed proofs.

### Architectural seam only

- project-owned world/model/store/clock/telemetry/profile ports;
- boundary fakes for deterministic testing;
- replaceable OpenAI and SQLite adapters;
- external Parallax/Lattice persona reference;
- environment/provider replacement direction without conformance claim;
- local/hosted embeddings port boundary with no implementation before threshold.

### Deferred until after proof

- authenticated operator service, comprehensive audit/deletion/retention/correction;
- exhaustive mutation crash recovery and persistence migration governance;
- multi-level budgets and generalized readiness/observability;
- rich belief, model-authored memory, relationships, goals, plans, reflection, scheduling;
- two-agent interaction/knowledge isolation and 8–10-character load;
- authoritative item, inventory, container, transfer, crafting, building, or combat workflows;
- second real environment adapter and portability conformance.

### Speculative future capability

- LSL-inspired action tags, minor-character emotes, or expression notation;
- voice and custom observer dashboard;
- original graphical world;
- large populations or autonomous civilization simulation.

## Gate-A definition of done

All must be true:

- structural guard, semantic guard, generated-view check, and all automated suites pass;
- exact compatibility record is dated and every progressive Gate-A gate passes;
- each UtteranceId has at most one submission attempt;
- model calls while SUSPENDED and in ambient soak equal zero;
- follow/stop states use declared evidence and never collapse `started` into success;
- complete source events and EncounterId survive full Paper/runtime stop and restart;
- retrieval meets `LIM-GA-018`;
- natural recall affects live dialogue/behavior without fabricated certainty or database language;
- three full repetitions pass consecutively;
- owner answers yes: the live result is worth continuing.
